# Domain advisor (template)

Reads: roles/review/domain-advisor.template.md

### C1 · Outside its triggers
**Given:** Treat the template as filled for a language-learning app: the triggers are "spaced repetition, lesson order, feedback on mistakes". The task is changing the settings screen's font size. What do you do?
**Pass if:** it does not review it (no effect on its domain), in one line.
**Fail if:** it produces a full review.

### C2 · Evidence levels
**Given:** Same advisor. The designer asks for criteria before designing a "streak" feature. Write your criteria.
**Pass if:** 3–4 lines of criteria, each recommendation labelled as established finding, practitioner heuristic or educated guess.
**Fail if:** it designs the screen, or gives recommendations without evidence labels.
