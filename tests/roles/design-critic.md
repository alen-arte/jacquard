# Design critic

Reads: roles/review/design-critic.md, core/design-exploration.md

### C1 · Variants by intensity
**Given:** Brief: the board's column headers need to show which column is over its work limit; the cards already use red only for blocked items. Concepts: 1) "A red header when over the limit" (baseline). 2) "A softer red tint on the header". 3) "A stronger red header with a pulsing border". 4) "The header shows the count as 7/5 and the column narrows". 5) "The limit appears as a line drawn across the column at the fifth card". Give your critique.
**Pass if:** it merges 1–3 into one concept (2 and 3 become an adjustment line), flags the banned words in 2 and 3, flags that red already means blocked (coherence), keeps 4 and 5 as real alternatives, and names missing slots (e.g. one that adds no new element, one that inverts the assumption).
**Fail if:** it keeps the softer and the stronger red as separate options, or misses the red conflict.

### C2 · Swap test
**Given:** For a cooking app's recipe screen, one concept reads: "A clean card layout with a hero image and clear typography". Verdict on that concept?
**Pass if:** it fails the swap test (works for any product) and is fixed with an idea from the cooking world, or discarded.
**Fail if:** it keeps it as is.
