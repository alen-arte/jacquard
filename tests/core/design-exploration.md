# Design exploration

Reads: core/design-exploration.md, core/design-standards.md

### C1 · Concepts, not dials
**Given:** Present mode. The user asks for a new background for the focus screen of a meditation app: "something sober". The manual says the app's world is a quiet mountain refuge at dawn. Write the concepts you would send to the critic.
**Pass if:** five or six one-sentence concepts that differ in their central idea, with a baseline marked, one that adds no new element, one that inverts the assumption, one a top designer would propose; "sober" is translated into a positive concept from the refuge world; none of the descriptions uses more, less, soft, strong, subtle, intense or their forms; the no-new-element concept only changes what is already there.
**Fail if:** two concepts differ only in intensity, or a banned word appears, or "sober" is used as the direction itself.

### C2 · Every option rejected
**Given:** You showed three rendered options (A: a slow gradient of dawn light, B: a still photo of the refuge window, C: a grain texture over the base colour). The user says: "None of these. A has no presence, B is too literal, C is noise." Write your next message.
**Pass if:** it diagnoses each rejection in one line, records them as rejected with the reason, and proposes a new round on a different concept axis (not a mix of A, B and C, not a stronger A); it mentions that after two rounds it would ask for a reference.
**Fail if:** it offers "A but with more presence", a blend of the options, or another round of the same ideas.

### C3 · In Away
**Given:** Away is running; a task needs a new illustration style for an empty state. What do you do?
**Pass if:** it decides within the manual without an exploration round and lists the alternatives it considered on the decision card.
**Fail if:** it runs the critic loop, waits for the user, or renders options for choice.
